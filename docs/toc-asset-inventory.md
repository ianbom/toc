## Editorial restrictions

- Six service illustrations are used as service/category imagery, not as independently verified product models.
- Chester Model U255 / 5 Axis Machine Center is one source-backed equipment entry, not three invented products.
- Earthquake Simulator Unit is sourced from an informational TOC article; availability is not asserted.
- The anniversary article is archived as published on April 15, 2025; its 46-year headline and 1978 founding assertion conflict with the homepage's 1977 statement. Corporate copy omits disputed chronology.
- Both Jakarta telephone numbers are published: header 625-1690, footer 625-1689. No invented department assignment or WhatsApp claim.
- Customer logos appear under Customer Kami on the source; no testimonials or endorsements are inferred. Partner logos are labeled as brands shown by TOC, not certifications.
- CDAKB imagery is not presented as proof of currently valid certification.
- The hero uses TOC's own 1920px city hero. No high-resolution clinical/company photograph was found; 667px product images are not stretched into full-screen photographs.
